Method

How I keep complex work from becoming vague work.

01

Map the pressure, not just the screen.

Enterprise users rarely fail because they cannot find a button. They fail because the product ignores approval chains, incentives, exceptions, and time pressure.

02

Make the decision visible.

Good enterprise UX explains enough of the system's reasoning for users to trust it, challenge it, and recover when reality refuses to be clean.

03

Prototype close to the final behavior.

I am using AI-assisted development to make sharper prototypes and learn the implementation tradeoffs earlier, where design decisions are still cheap to change.
